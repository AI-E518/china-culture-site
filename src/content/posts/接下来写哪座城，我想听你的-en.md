Let me start with full honesty: I wrote all these articles myself—just one laptop and over two decades of traveling across China. But there are only so many hours in a day, and the cities worth writing about, the foods worth savoring, and the vanishing crafts worth documenting far outnumber what any single person could ever cover.

So far, I’ve gone deepest into **Beijing and Shanghai**:  
- Beijing: six full days  
- Shanghai: one full day, plus seven deep-dive pieces—  
  • Breakfast at 6 a.m.  
  • Is Shanghainese food *really* sweet?  
  • How does tax-free shopping actually work?  
  • What bags are people bringing home these days?  
  • Two rivers (Suzhou Creek & Huangpu River)  
  • Which museums close on which days  
  • And my personal favorite: November  

I’ve also covered roast duck, lamb spine hotpot, and pear-syrup candy.

Next, I’ll move city by city—covering China’s major urban centers one at a time.  
**But which city comes first? I want *you* to decide.**

Which city fascinates you most? Or rather than yet another list of tourist spots, what do you *actually* want to read?  
**Food? Unique local culture? Intangible cultural heritage on the verge of disappearing? A custom travel itinerary—tailored just for you?**  
Tell me what speaks to you—and reply to let me know.  
**One reply = one vote. I’ll count them myself.**

Just four lines in your reply:  
Where you’re from  
Roughly when you’ll visit  
How many days you’ll stay  
What you most want to understand  

I’ll read every single reply—and draw on my two decades of on-the-ground experience to give you honest, practical advice: where to go, where to skip, and how to pack it all into your tight schedule.  
I’m not a travel agency—and I can’t promise same-day replies—but I *will* reply.

**But I don’t want those answers to stay buried in your inbox.**  
If you’re comfortable, I’ll anonymize your question and post my answer publicly—keeping only your country, omitting your name and email. The most-asked questions will become a recurring column: **“You Ask, I Run.”** Next time someone asks the same thing, they’ll just need to read that piece.

Each month, I’ll update this pinned page with the vote tally: which city and which topic got the most votes—and that’s exactly what my next article will be.  
**You’ll literally watch your own message turn into a published article.**

And where you usually ask questions—Reddit, Facebook groups, Quora—I’ll be there too, under the same name, giving the same thoughtful replies. Say hello if you spot me.

One small request: Be specific.  
“I love food” doesn’t help me much.  
But “I’m visiting Shanghai for four days in March—and I want to eat breakfast *like a local Shanghainese person*”—that? That I can work with.

So—the next city is up to you.  

See you there.  

**Lao Ai**  
Written in Shanghai
