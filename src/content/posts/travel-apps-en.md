Walk into China with the right eight apps and the country feels effortless. Walk in relying on the Western stack you use at home, and you'll hit a wall on day one. Here's the shortlist that actually matters — and what to stop depending on.

## The must-install shortlist
1. **Alipay (支付宝)** — your all-in-one: pay at shops, pull up the metro/bus QR code, and open mini-programs for everything from attractions to hospital registration. Set it up before you fly (passport verification needed).
2. **WeChat (微信)** — messaging, WeChat Pay, and a universe of mini-programs (bookings, government services, group chats). If a local sends you a link, it opens here.
3. **Amap / Baidu Maps (高德 / 百度地图)** — China's real navigation. Accurate metro lines, walking routes, and live traffic. **Google Maps is unreliable here — don't count on it.**
4. **DiDi (滴滴)** — the local ride-hailing app, with an English interface and Alipay/WeChat payment. Safer and cheaper than street hails.
5. **Trip.com (携程)** — hotels, trains, flights, and attraction tickets in English, with foreign-card payment. The visitor-friendly alternative to 12306.
6. **Railway 12306 (铁路 12306)** — official train ticketing. Use if you want the cheapest fares and don't mind the strict name matching; otherwise let Trip.com handle it.
7. **Pleco** — an offline Chinese–English dictionary with camera lookup. Lifesaver for menus and signs. Your phone's built-in translator works too.
8. **Meituan / Ele.me (美团 / 饿了么)** — food delivery and local deals. Optional, but great for late-night cravings and group dinners.

> **Install-before-you-fly checklist**: Alipay, WeChat, Amap, DiDi, Trip.com, 12306, Pleco. Do the Alipay/WeChat passport verification at home on unrestricted Wi-Fi.

## What to stop relying on
- **Google Maps** → use Amap/Baidu.
- **Gmail / Google services** → access depends on your connection (use an international eSIM or roaming to keep them).
- **WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook / YouTube / X** → blocked without a workaround. WeChat covers chat; for the rest, arrange access before departure.

## Nice-to-have
- **RedNote (Xiaohongshu / 小红书)** — China's lifestyle discovery app; brilliant for restaurant and photo-spot ideas, though it's Chinese-language heavy. Fun to browse with your translator.
- **Baidu Translate / Tencent Translate** — camera translation for signs and menus when Pleco isn't enough.
- **Apple Translate** — works offline and handles Mandarin well.

## One account to rule them
Most of these hinge on **Alipay + WeChat**, both set up with your passport. Nail those two at home and the other six are just downloads. You don't need a Chinese number for the essentials — an international eSIM keeps everything running.

Eight apps. Two set up properly. The rest of China just… works.
